    3. Android phone (payment detector)

    …
    1. Use the phone that gets your bank SMS. Any internet connection works; it talks to the public tunnel.
    2. Scan Download app with the phone camera and install it (allow "install unknown apps").
    3. Open the YOUPI Observer app, scan Pair this phone, then tap Pair.
    4. Grant notification access (and SMS access if asked).

    5. Test a payment

    1. Open the demo store (works from any device) and buy something for ₹1.
    2. Log in with the WhatsApp code, then pay the QR from a different UPI account.
    3. Tap "Payment done". The Android detector sees the bank alert and the store shows success.
    4. Watch it live in Merchant Admin.
